# CrewSphere marketing website

A self-contained responsive marketing website concept for a security-workforce SaaS platform.

## Brand positioning
**Tagline:** Manage Your Workforce. Control Every Shift.

**One-liner:** An all-in-one workforce and field operations platform for managing teams, shifts, compliance, reporting, clients, and billing.

## Open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Working brand
The site currently uses **CrewSphere** as a working name because the user's preferred **GuardSphere** name is already in active commercial use. All brand text is plain HTML and can be replaced globally once a final name is selected.

## Included sections
- Product positioning and operations dashboard concept
- Scheduling, attendance, patrol/checkpoints, duties and incident reporting
- Photo-verified patrol tasks and live-picture verification
- AI-assisted reporting
- Employee management, documents, licenses/certifications, KPI/performance and self-service
- Mobile field app concept
- Client portal and service requests
- Billing, invoicing and payments
- Equipment and access management
- API, integrations and webhooks
- Role-based experiences
- Recommended SaaS pricing tiers and add-ons
- FAQ and call-to-action

Pricing is illustrative launch packaging and should be validated against customer interviews and unit economics before publication.
