FAQ

Questions buyers will ask before they book a demo.

Is CrewSphere only for security guard companies?

The working positioning is security-first because patrols, checkpoints, post instructions, incident reporting and guard verification are core workflows. The workforce layer can later support adjacent field-service and staffing use cases.

Can clients see data from other customers?

No. The product should enforce tenant and client-level separation so a client only sees the sites, officers, reports, invoices, documents and users explicitly assigned to its organization.

Can officers submit reports using AI?

AI can help improve grammar, structure and professionalism, but the officer reviews, edits and approves the report. The AI should not automatically submit a report on the officer's behalf.

How does photo verification work?

Selected checkpoints can require a fresh in-app photo, and random live-picture requests can be issued during a shift. Gallery uploads can be blocked where live evidence is required.

Can the platform generate client invoices?

Yes. Billing can use approved shift hours, client/site rates, overtime/holiday rules and custom cycles, with review and approval before invoices are sent.

Can we integrate accounting, payroll or payment tools?

The product scope supports payment gateways, REST APIs, webhooks and integration frameworks for accounting, payroll, communications, maps and calendars.
