Frequently asked questions

Security workforce software, explained.

What is security workforce management software?

It helps security operators manage scheduling, attendance, patrol verification, incident reporting, workforce compliance, client visibility and billing from one connected system.

Can ForceVera manage QR patrols and checkpoints?

Yes. Patrol routes can include QR checkpoints, timestamps, missed-checkpoint tracking, task requirements and photo evidence.

Can clients see their own reports and activity?

Yes. Authorized client users can access assigned sites, activity, reports, incidents, service requests and billing information without seeing other clients' data.

Does ForceVera support mobile field operations?

Yes. The mobile experience covers active shifts, attendance, patrols, duties, reports, live verification and employee self-service.
